V. Zenith
Co-ordinator Pragnya 2k26
JNTUH University, Kukatpally

As the student coordinator for Pragnya 2k26 organized by the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) at JNTUH College, V. Zenith has exhibited exceptional leadership and organizational skills. His commitment to ensuring the success of the event has been evident through meticulous planning and execution. Zenith has played a pivotal role in coordinating various aspects of Pragnya 2k26, such as managing event schedules, overseeing logistical arrangements, and facilitating effective communication among the participating teams.

Zenith's dedication to promoting a collaborative and vibrant academic environment has significantly contributed to the success of Pragnya 2k26. His ability to efficiently multitask and handle diverse responsibilities has not only streamlined the event planning process but has also fostered a sense of unity and enthusiasm among his fellow students. Under Zenith's guidance, Pragnya 2k26 promises to be a platform that not only celebrates academic excellence but also provides a memorable and engaging experience for all participants.

At Pragnya, we anticipate a captivating and enlightening session as V. Zenith shares his insights and experiences, inspiring attendees to explore the dynamic world of technology. His presence adds immense value to our technical festival, promising an engaging discourse on the latest advancements and challenges in the realm of technology. We look forward to the privilege of learning from Zenith's expertise and benefiting from his valuable perspectives at Pragnya.
